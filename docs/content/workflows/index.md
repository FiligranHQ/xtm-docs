A journey takes you to one outcome, such as checking your defenses against the threats you track or rehearsing a crisis, through the pages of every product it needs, in order. Open a journey to read what it achieves, who takes part and what you need, then review its steps before you start: once started, each of its pages shows where you are and the next step.

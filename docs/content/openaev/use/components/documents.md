A document is a file you upload to OpenAEV once and reuse wherever the exercise needs it. It can be a briefing attached to an email, a photo in a fake press article, the evidence of a challenge, or the logo of a media channel. Validation engineers use the same documents as the files a file drop action writes on endpoints. This page uploads a document, attaches it to injects, and tidies the list.

## Before you start

- Your role has **Access documents** to see the list, **Manage documents** to upload and update, and **Delete documents** to delete.
- To attach a document to an inject or an article, your role also has **Manage assessment** on the scenario or simulation, or your group holds its **Manage+Delete** grant.

## Where a document goes

::diagram{id=openaev-documents-uses}

| Used in | What the players or the platform do with it |
| --- | --- |
| An inject that sends email, such as **Publish a media pressure** or **Publish challenges** | The file goes with the email when its attachment is turned on |
| A media pressure article | The image or the video shows in the article; see [Publish fake press articles during an exercise](/openaev/use/components/media-pressure/) |
| A challenge | The players download it from the challenge card; see [Give your players challenges to solve](/openaev/use/components/challenges/) |
| A channel | Its logo, for the dark and the light theme; see [Create the media outlets of an exercise](/openaev/use/components/channels/) |
| A payload of the Threat Arsenal | The file a file drop action writes on the endpoint; see [Threat arsenal](/openaev/use/threat-arsenal/) |

## Upload a document

:::steps

1. Open :menu[Components > Documents] and click **Create**. The **Create a new document** panel opens.
2. Under **Select a file**, choose the file on your computer.
3. Fill in a **Description**: the players never see it, but your team finds the file by it.
4. Optionally, link it to **Simulations** and **Scenarios**, and add **Tags**. Links file the document under those exercises in the list; they do not send it to anyone.
5. Click **Create**.

:::

The document appears in the list with its file name as **Name**, and its file type, such as `application/pdf` or `image/png`, as **Type**. Click its line to download the file.

The **Simulations** and **Scenarios** columns show, on one line, the first simulation or scenario the document is linked to: click its chip to open it. A chip such as `+2` counts the other links, and hovering over it lists their names. Only the simulations and scenarios you have access to are shown and counted.

From inside an inject, an article or a challenge, **Add documents** offers the same upload with **Create a new document**. The document is then linked to the scenario or simulation you work in.

OpenAEV recognizes a file by its content. Uploading a file whose content is already in the list does not create a second document. OpenAEV adds your tags to the existing one and keeps its name and description.

::screenshot{id=openaev-documents-list alt="The Documents list with the three files of the Velvet Harbor crisis night exercise, each with its description, type and the tag velvet-harbor. The crisis communication playbook serves two scenarios: one scenario chip and a +1 chip."}

## Attach a document to an inject

:::steps

1. Open the scenario or the simulation, then its **Injects** tab, and click the inject to open its form.
2. Click **Add documents**, select one or more documents, and click **Add**.
3. Check the attachment of each document. For an inject that sends email, a new document is attached: it goes with the email. Open the menu of a document and choose **Disable attachment** to keep it on the inject without sending it, or **Enable attachment** to send it again.
4. Save the inject.

:::

**Remove from the element**, in the same menu, takes the document off the inject and keeps it in :menu[Components > Documents].

## Keep the list tidy

- **Update**, in the menu of a line, changes the description, the links and the tags. The file itself cannot be replaced: upload the new version as a new document.
- **Delete** first lists where the document is used: atomic testings, injects of scenarios and simulations, simulations, payloads, channels, articles, challenges and security platforms. A document used in a payload, or as the logo a collector uploaded for a security platform, cannot be deleted. Elsewhere, deleting removes the file from every inject, article and challenge that used it.
- Report files generated by the **Reporting** module show in the list but are read-only.
- **Export this list**, the icon above the list, downloads it as a CSV file.

:::example[The documents of the Grand Meridian crisis exercise]
Example CERT prepares the crisis exercise it runs for Grand Meridian Hotels on the night of 9 January 2026. In the scenario **Velvet Harbor crisis night**, the exercise planner uploads three documents and links them to the scenario:

| Name | Description | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `crisis-communication-playbook.pdf` | Grand Meridian crisis communication playbook, version of December 2025 | Attached to the first email to the crisis cell |
| `forum-listing-grand-meridian.png` | Fake screenshot of the guest records offered for sale | Shown in the press article of 23:30 |
| `edr-alert-front-desk.pdf` | Meridian EDR alert exported from the front-desk workstation | Downloaded from the challenge **Find the account that was used** |

Each document is uploaded once. When the planner duplicates the scenario for the Spanish hotels, the copied injects and articles point to the same files, and the challenge is shared. The copy is linked to the same documents: in :menu[Components > Documents], the **Scenarios** column of the playbook shows one of the two scenarios and a `+1` chip that names the other.
:::

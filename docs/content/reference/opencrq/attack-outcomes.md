An outcome is what the attacker is after, not how they get in: the same intrusion can end in ransomware or in espionage. A risk scenario names one outcome, and OpenCRQ prices it from the attacks that reach its tactic on your primary assets. Read [how OpenCRQ quantifies risk](/opencrq/concepts/how-risk-is-quantified/) for the attack run.

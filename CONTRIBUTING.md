# Contributing

Thank you for helping us improve the XTM documentation.

**Open an issue.** This repository is a one-way mirror of the documentation's sources: pull requests here cannot be
merged, and are closed automatically. An issue reaches the documentation team, who writes the change, has it reviewed
and publishes it.

- **From a page** of [filigran.io/docs](https://filigran.io/docs/): **Give us feedback**, or select the passage that is
  wrong and click **Report an issue**. The issue comes filled with the page, the version it describes and the passage.
- **From here**: [a new issue](https://github.com/FiligranHQ/xtm-docs/issues/new/choose), with the page's address.

**Members of FiligranHQ** have the documentation factory make the change from the issue itself: comment `/doc` and
what the page should say; the factory answers with a draft (signed in with your Filigran account). Each comment of a
member is another round on that draft; `/merge` publishes it once its checks passed, `/abandon` drops it. Comments
from outside FiligranHQ are not passed to the factory: a member who takes up a reader's issue writes the change in
their own words after `/doc`.

What helps:
- the page, and the version of the product you use;
- what is wrong or missing, and what you expected;
- for a step that fails, what you did and what the product showed.

Not here:
- a bug or a feature request of a product: its own repository
  ([OpenCTI](https://github.com/OpenCTI-Platform/opencti/issues),
  [OpenAEV](https://github.com/OpenAEV-Platform/openaev/issues),
  [XTM One](https://github.com/XTM-One-Platform/xtm-one/issues));
- a question about using a product: the [community](https://community.filigran.io);
- a security vulnerability: never in public, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
